## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Non-Competition Clause** (Section 11(a))
- **Issue:** Restricts employment in any competitive business worldwide for 12 months post-termination.
- **Risk:** Limits future employment opportunities significantly.
- **Market Standard:** Typically 6-12 months, limited geographic scope.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could be narrowed to specific regions or roles.
- **Suggested Redline:** Limit geographic scope or specify more narrowly defined competing businesses.

### 🟡 Important

**Termination Provisions** (Section 7)
- **Issue:** Broad definitions of "Cause" and "Good Reason" could lead to disputes.
- **Market Standard:** Clearer, more specific definitions.
- **Negotiability:** High — clarity benefits both parties.
- **Suggested Redline:** Specify examples or clearer thresholds for "Cause" and "Good Reason."
