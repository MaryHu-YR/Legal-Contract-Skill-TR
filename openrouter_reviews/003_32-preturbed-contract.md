## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Default terms section)
> "Maker shall pay Holder, as liquidated damages... an amount equal to twenty percent (20%) of the outstanding principal balance at the time of the failure."

- **Issue:** High liquidated damages which may be disproportionate to actual harm.
- **Risk:** Significant financial burden in case of default.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are closer to 10%.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could argue for a reduction based on industry norms.
- **Redline:** Reduce the liquidated damages clause to 10% of the outstanding principal.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Late Payment Terms** (Late payment terms section)
> "Any payment which is not paid within five (5) days of the date due... will bear interest at the Default Rate."

- **Issue:** High default interest rate and additional late fee.
- **Market Standard:** Late fees around 5% and default rates 2-3% above the standard rate.
- **Negotiability:** High — these terms are often negotiable.
- **Redline:** Reduce the late fee to 5% and the default interest rate to 11% (8% + 3%).
