## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 1)
> "Payment of $25,000,000 as liquidated damages for each breach"

- **Issue:** Extremely high liquidated damages could be financially burdensome.
- **Risk:** High financial exposure for any breach, regardless of actual damage.
- **Market Standard:** More moderate liquidated damages based on actual harm.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but worth discussing if further amendments occur.
- **Redline:** Suggest moderating the damages to align with actual potential harm.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Governing Law** (Section 2)
> "Governing by the laws of the State of New York"

- **Issue:** New York law may not be favorable in all respects for both parties.
- **Market Standard:** Often chosen for commercial agreements; generally acceptable.
- **Negotiability:** Low — common choice, but note for future negotiations.
