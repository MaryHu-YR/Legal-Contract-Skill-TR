## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Interest Rate Terms** (Article 2)
> Changes to interest calculations based on LPR

- **Issue:** Complexity and potential unpredictability in rate adjustments
- **Risk:** Could lead to higher costs if LPR increases significantly
- **Market Standard:** Typically, interest rates are fixed or cap-adjusted
- **Negotiability:** Medium
- **Redline:** Suggest adding a cap to the maximum rate increase per period

### 🟡 Important

**Liquidated Damages** (Article 3.1)
> High liquidated damages for non-performance

- **Issue:** The amount is substantial and may be punitive
- **Market Standard:** Lower or tiered liquidated damages
- **Negotiability:** High
- **Redline:** Propose reducing the amount or implementing a tiered system based on the severity of breach
