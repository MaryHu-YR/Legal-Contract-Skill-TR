## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Termination Provisions** (Section 6.1)
> "This Agreement shall terminate on the date on which the Offered Notes and any other amounts owed to the Agent, any Investor or any Indemnified Party under the Transaction Documents have been paid in full..."

- **Issue:** The amendment modifies the termination clause which could extend liabilities indefinitely based on the condition of full payment.
- **Risk:** Potential for prolonged financial obligations.
- **Market Standard:** Defined termination dates or events.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — subject to financial stability and performance.
- **Redline:** Suggest adding a clause to limit the duration or conditions under which the agreement remains effective.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Liability Limitations** (Section 9)
> "Under no circumstances shall Wilmington Trust, National Association be personally liable..."

- **Issue:** Explicit limitation of liability for Wilmington Trust, potentially shifting greater risk to other parties.
- **Market Standard:** Mutual limitation of liabilities.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — depends on the roles and responsibilities defined within the broader transaction framework.
- **Redline:** Propose mutual limitations of liability across all significant parties.
