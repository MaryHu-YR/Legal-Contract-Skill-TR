## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Reimbursement for Breach** (Section 2)
> In case of breach, Tenant must pay unamortized parking charges, a $25,000 administrative fee, and $2,500 per day until full payment.

- **Issue:** High penalties for any breach, including minor ones.
- **Risk:** Significant financial exposure from strict breach penalties.
- **Market Standard:** More lenient breach penalties.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed.
- **Redline:** N/A due to executed status.

### 🟡 Important

**Confidentiality Obligations** (Section 5)
> Tenant must not disclose any terms of the amendment.

- **Issue:** Broad confidentiality requirements with severe penalties for breach.
- **Market Standard:** Confidentiality with less severe penalties.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed.
