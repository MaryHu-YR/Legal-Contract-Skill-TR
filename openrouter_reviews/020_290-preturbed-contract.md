## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages for Breach**
> In the event of a breach, the undersigned must pay the greater of estimated losses or the market value of the securities plus $250,000 per day.

- **Issue:** Extremely punitive damages which could lead to significant financial liability.
- **Risk:** High financial risk if breach occurs.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are less punitive.
- **Negotiability:** Low — likely non-negotiable due to the nature of the agreement.
- **Redline:** Seek to cap the additional daily damages or reduce the base penalty.

### 🟡 Important

**Jurisdiction and Governing Law**
> The agreement is governed by New York law and subject to the jurisdiction of the Southern District of New York.

- **Issue:** May not be favorable depending on the undersigned's location.
- **Market Standard:** Common for U.S. agreements.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — possible to request a change if the undersigned has significant leverage.
- **Redline:** Propose a neutral jurisdiction or state of residence if outside New York.
