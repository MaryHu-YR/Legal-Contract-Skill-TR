# Contract Review: Independent Director Compensation Policy

**Document Type:** Compensation Policy
**Your Position:** N/A
**Counterparty:** SiTime Corporation
**Risk Level:** 🟢 Low
**Document Status:** Approved on July 27, 2020

## Executive Summary

This policy outlines the compensation for non-employee members of the board of directors of SiTime Corporation. It includes both cash and equity compensation, which are automatically granted unless declined by the director. The policy is comprehensive and appears standard for director compensation, including specific provisions for different committee roles and initial as well as annual equity grants.

## Risk Analysis

### 🟢 Reviewed & Acceptable

**Cash Compensation**
- **Status:** Standard rates for board and committee service.
- **Notes:** Aligns with market standards for director compensation.

**Equity Compensation**
- **Status:** Clearly defined RSU awards with standard vesting conditions.
- **Notes:** Includes provisions for acceleration upon a Change in Control.

**Annual Limitations**
- **Status:** Caps on total compensation to avoid excessive payouts.
- **Notes:** Limits are high enough to attract and retain quality directors but capped to prevent unreasonable compensation.
