## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages Clause** (Section 8.4)
> "Any underpayment or delay in making such payment shall automatically give rise to liquidated damages in an amount equal to the greater of (i) such unpaid or delayed amount plus interest, or (ii) $250,000 for each instance of nonpayment..."

- **Issue:** High penalty for nonpayment could be seen as punitive.
- **Risk:** Could deter amicable resolution of payment disputes.
- **Market Standard:** Interest on late payments without high fixed penalties.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could be adjusted to a less punitive measure.
- **Redline:** Suggest reducing fixed penalty or relying on interest calculation.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Extended Notice Period for Termination** (Section 11.1)
> "From thirty (30) Business Days to sixty (60) Business Days."

- **Issue:** Longer notice period could delay Dunn's ability to transition quickly to new opportunities.
- **Market Standard:** 30-60 days notice.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to adjust based on role.
- **Redline:** Confirm alignment with personal career flexibility needs.
