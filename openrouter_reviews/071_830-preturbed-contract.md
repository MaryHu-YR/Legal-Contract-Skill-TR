## Executive Summary

This document amends the Nonqualified Stock Option Agreement under Avantor, Inc.'s Equity Incentive Plan. The amendment extends the exercisability of vested options to the tenth anniversary of the grant date but introduces a clause that allows for immediate expiration of options upon noncompliance with company policies, at the company's sole discretion.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Termination and Expiration of Vested Options** (Section 1(a))
> "In the event of the Participant’s Termination, the Vested Portion shall remain exercisable until the tenth (10th) anniversary of the Date of Grant; provided, however, that if the Company determines in its sole discretion that the Participant failed to comply with any provision of the Grant, the Plan, or any Company policy, the Vested Portion shall expire immediately upon such noncompliance..."

- **Issue:** The company has the sole discretion to determine noncompliance, which can lead to immediate expiration of vested options.
- **Risk:** High potential for disputes and loss of vested options without clear recourse.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, there are more defined processes and standards for determining noncompliance.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but for future amendments, seek more balanced language.
- **Redline:** N/A due to executed status.
- **Fallback:** For future agreements, suggest adding specific definitions of noncompliance and a formal review process before options can expire.
