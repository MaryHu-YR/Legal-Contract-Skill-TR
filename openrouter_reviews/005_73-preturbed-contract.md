## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 1)
> "The Company shall pay to each Purchaser...an amount equal to one and one-half percent (1.5%) of the aggregate purchase price paid by such Purchaser for its Registrable Securities for each thirty (30) day period...during which such failure continues."

- **Issue:** Automatic liquidated damages for delays could be costly.
- **Risk:** Financial penalty without opportunity to cure.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, some cure period is allowed.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could argue for a cure period before penalties apply.
- **Redline:** Add a cure period of 10-15 days before liquidated damages apply.
