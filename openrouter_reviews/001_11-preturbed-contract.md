## Executive Summary

This agreement outlines the terms for the award of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) to a non-employee director under Barrett Business Services, Inc.'s 2020 Stock Incentive Plan. The RSUs vest 100% at the end of a one-year restriction period, with provisions for accelerated vesting in specific circumstances. The agreement is standard for non-employee director incentives but requires clarity on the exact number of RSUs and their initial value.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Clawback/Recovery Provisions** (Section 9)
> Subject to recoupment under corporate clawback policy and applicable laws.

- **Issue:** Broad and potentially onerous recovery obligations.
- **Risk:** Participant may have to return compensation under various circumstances beyond their control.
- **Market Standard:** Typically narrower in scope.
- **Negotiability:** Low — driven by regulatory requirements.
- **Redline:** Seek clarification on specific scenarios under which clawback would be triggered.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Arbitration Clause** (Section 7)
> Mandatory arbitration in Multnomah County, Oregon.

- **Issue:** Limits dispute resolution to arbitration, which can restrict legal recourse.
- **Market Standard:** Common in corporate agreements but often negotiable.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — possible to request judicial recourse options.
- **Redline:** Propose addition of an option for mediation prior to arbitration or a clause allowing for small claims court actions.
