## Executive Summary

This side letter agreement modifies the existing Chugach Eklutna Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) between Chugach Electric Association, Inc. and the Municipality of Anchorage. It addresses the allocation of the Seller’s Interest during an interim period not originally contemplated by the main PPA. The agreement introduces a significant liquidated damages clause for non-compliance.
