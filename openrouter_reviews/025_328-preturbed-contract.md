## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Salary Increase Contingency** (Amendment Section 3(a))
> "The amount of the Increase... shall be accrued until the closing of the Business Combination Agreement"

- **Issue:** Salary increase is contingent upon the closing of another agreement, which may not occur.
- **Risk:** Potential delay or non-receipt of increased salary.
- **Market Standard:** Guaranteed salary increases without external contingencies.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to request removal of contingency or provision of a guaranteed minimum increase.
- **Redline:** Remove reference to the Business Combination Agreement or ensure a minimum guaranteed increase regardless of external factors.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Liquidated Damages Clause** (Amendment Section 3(a))
> "Upon any failure to comply with this Section 3(a)... the Company shall pay Executive as liquidated damages an amount equal to all accrued but unpaid Increase amounts plus an additional $250,000"

- **Issue:** High penalty for non-compliance may be seen as punitive.
- **Market Standard:** Liquidated damages typically reflect estimated actual damages.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — clarify basis for the amount or adjust to more closely reflect potential damages.
- **Redline:** Modify the liquidated damages clause to align with realistic estimates of actual damages.
