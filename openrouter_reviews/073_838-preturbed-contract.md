## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Financial Obligations and Penalties** (Section 4)
> "Investor shall pay to the Company, as liquidated damages...an amount equal to ten percent (10%) of the Purchase Amount requested..."

- **Issue:** High penalty for non-performance which could be seen as punitive.
- **Risk:** Could deter the Investor from fulfilling obligations or lead to disputes.
- **Market Standard:** Lower or more proportional penalties.
- **Negotiability:** Medium
- **Redline:** Reduce penalty to 5% or link to actual damages.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Expense Reimbursement** (Section 6)
> "Company shall cause to be paid to the Investor, $120,000 for reimbursement of expenses..."

- **Issue:** Fixed expense reimbursement regardless of actual expenses.
- **Risk:** Overpayment if actual expenses are lower.
- **Market Standard:** Reimbursement based on actual and reasonable expenses.
- **Negotiability:** High
- **Redline:** Change to "reasonable and documented expenses not to exceed $120,000."
