## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Paragraph 4)
> "QuantumScape shall pay VWGoAI, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to $134 million for any such breach or failure, plus $5 million per day..."

- **Issue:** Extremely high liquidated damages, potentially disproportionate to actual harm.
- **Risk:** Could be seen as punitive, risking enforceability.
- **Market Standard:** More reasonable and proportionate liquidated damages.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but noteworthy for future negotiations.
- **Redline:** Suggest more reasonable damages based on actual harm in future agreements.
