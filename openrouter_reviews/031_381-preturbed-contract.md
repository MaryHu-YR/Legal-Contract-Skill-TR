## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Conversion Price Adjustment** (Sections 1(a), 2(a))
> "Conversion price equal to $0.40 per share."

- **Issue:** Significant reduction from previous $1.00/$1.50 to $0.40, aligning with a new stock offering price.
- **Risk:** Could dilute existing shareholders significantly if large amounts are converted.
- **Market Standard:** Typically aligned with market conditions but warrants scrutiny due to potential impact.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — depends on the financial condition of the company and market response.
- **Redline:** Consider a staggered conversion rate or protective provisions against excessive dilution.

### 🟡 Important

**Liquidated Damages** (Sections 1(a), 2(a))
> "Pay to Lender as liquidated damages an amount equal to the greater of (x) $50,000 or (y) fifteen percent (15%) of the then-outstanding principal..."

- **Issue:** Potentially high liquidated damages could strain the company if triggered.
- **Market Standard:** Liquidated damages are common, but amounts are typically negotiated based on risk exposure.
- **Negotiability:** High — should align with actual potential damages.
- **Redline:** Cap the damages or tie them more closely to actual damages incurred.
