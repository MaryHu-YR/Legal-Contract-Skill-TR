## Executive Summary

This agreement outlines the terms for a Performance Share Unit (PSU) Award under the Regal Beloit Corporation 2018 Equity Incentive Plan. The award is contingent on the company's return on invested capital (ROIC) and other metrics at the discretion of the Administrator. The document grants significant discretionary power to the Administrator, particularly concerning performance metrics, PSU adjustments, and termination outcomes, which could be seen as unfavorable from the recipient's perspective.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Administrator's Discretion**
> The Administrator has broad discretion to adjust performance metrics and determine the earning of PSUs.

- **Issue:** Excessive control could lead to unpredictable outcomes for the PSU earning.
- **Risk:** Potential for unfavorable adjustments or cancellations of PSUs without recipient's input.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, some level of employee input or more defined criteria are expected.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — Clarification and limitation of discretion could be requested.
- **Redline:** Propose adding specific criteria for adjustments and a consultation process with recipients.

### 🟡 Important

**Change of Control**
> Treatment of the Award upon a Change of Control is determined by the Plan or Administrator.

- **Issue:** Lack of clarity on how PSUs will be treated, which could lead to cancellation without compensation.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, acceleration of vesting or protection against cancellation without compensation.
- **Negotiability:** High — Reasonable to request specific terms for Change of Control scenarios.
- **Redline:** Add provisions for accelerated vesting or fair compensation upon Change of Control.
