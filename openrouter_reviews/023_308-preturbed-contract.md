## Executive Summary

This agreement outlines the conditions under which founder holders will forfeit shares of dMY Class A Common Stock in connection with a business combination agreement. The forfeiture is contingent on the cash amounts involved in the transaction, with a detailed forfeiture mechanism based on the cash thresholds.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Transfer Restrictions** (Section 4)
> Prohibits transfer of shares prior to final determination of forfeited shares.

- **Issue:** Restricts liquidity and financial flexibility of founder holders.
- **Risk:** Founder holders cannot divest or leverage their shares during the determination period.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, transfer restrictions are less stringent.
- **Negotiability:** Low — likely non-negotiable due to the nature of the agreement.
- **Redline:** N/A due to executed status.

### 🟡 Important

**Forfeiture Mechanism** (Sections 1 and 2)
> Automatic forfeiture based on "Minimum Cash" thresholds.

- **Issue:** Complex forfeiture calculation could lead to disputes or unintended consequences.
