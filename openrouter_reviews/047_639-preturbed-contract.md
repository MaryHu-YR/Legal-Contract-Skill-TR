## Executive Summary

This is a standard loan agreement under the Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) facilitated by the SBA. It includes provisions for loan forgiveness based on specific uses of the loan amount, aligning with the CARES Act requirements. The interest rate is favorable at 1%, but the liquidated damages clause for defaults is notably stringent.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages for Default** (Section 5)
> "Borrower shall pay...an amount equal to twenty percent (20%) of the unpaid principal balance for each month the default continues..."

- **Issue:** Extremely punitive liquidated damages clause
- **Risk:** Could lead to substantial financial liability beyond principal and interest in case of default
- **Market Standard:** More typical default penalties are less severe and based on actual damages
- **Negotiability:** Low — given the standardized nature of PPP loans, but worth discussing with lender if possible
- **Redline:** Suggest modifying to a less severe penalty based on actual damages incurred by the lender
