## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Delay Charge** (Section 5A)
> "Seller shall pay Buyer a delay charge equal to the greater of (i) US$25,000,000 per day from the first day of the Scheduled Delivery Period until actual delivery, or (ii) 10% of the aggregate purchase price of the Aircraft then affected..."

- **Issue:** Extremely high penalties which may not be enforceable or could be viewed as punitive.
- **Risk:** Potential for legal disputes over the enforceability of these terms.
- **Market Standard:** Liquidated damages are common, but typically not at this scale.
