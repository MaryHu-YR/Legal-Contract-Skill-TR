## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Termination Fee and Liquidated Damages** (Section 6)
> "Tenant shall pay to Landlord the sum of $345,867.60 on the Effective Date... an additional amount equal to the Termination Fee for each breach..."

- **Issue:** High termination fee and additional liquidated damages that are uncapped.
- **Risk:** Significant financial exposure if terms are not met precisely.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, termination fees are capped and more predictable.
- **Negotiability:** N/A (already executed)
- **Suggestion:** Future agreements should cap potential damages and clarify conditions.

### 🟡 Important

**Holdover Penalties** (Section 9)
> "Basic Rent and Additional Rent equal to two hundred percent (200%) of the rate in effect for the prior rental period; plus any consequential damages incurred by Landlord..."

- **Issue:** Excessive penalties for holdover beyond the Termination Date.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, holdover rent is 125%-150% of the regular rent.
- **Negotiability:** N/A (already executed)
- **Suggestion:** Future agreements should negotiate lower holdover penalties.
