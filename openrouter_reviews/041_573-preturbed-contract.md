## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 10)
> "If Director fails to comply with any provision of this Agreement... the Company may require Director to pay liquidated damages in the amount of $50,000 per occurrence, plus $2,500 for each day such failure continues..."

- **Issue:** Extremely punitive liquidated damages clause without opportunity for cure
- **Risk:** High financial risk from minor breaches
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are not this severe without a cure period
- **Negotiability:** Medium — should negotiate for a cure period or reduction in damages
- **Redline:** Modify to include a reasonable cure period and reduce per-day penalties
- **Fallback:** Cap the total liquidated damages or link them to actual damages

---

### 🟡 Important

**Mutual Non-Disparagement** (Section 4)
> "Director and the Company mutually agree to forbear from making disparaging remarks..."

- **Issue:** Could limit freedom of speech regarding legitimate company issues
- **Market Standard:** Non-disparagement clauses are common but should be mutual and reasonable
- **Negotiability:** High — clarify what constitutes "disparaging remarks"
- **Redline:** Define "disparaging remarks" to ensure it does not cover protected whistleblower activities
