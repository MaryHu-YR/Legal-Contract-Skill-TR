## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Exclusive and Non-Exclusive Licenses** (Sections a.ii, a.iii)
- **Issue:** Changes in licensing terms could impact control and revenue from the licensed compounds and products.
- **Risk:** Restrictive licensing could limit Lumos's ability to operate freely and capitalize on the compounds.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, license agreements provide balanced rights that safeguard both parties' interests.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — Changes to licensing terms should be carefully negotiated to ensure mutual benefits.
- **Redline:** Seek to balance the exclusivity terms to ensure competitive advantage while maintaining flexibility.

### 🟡 Important

**Sublicense Agreements and Enforcement Rights** (Section a.vi)
- **Issue:** The requirement for Merck’s prior written consent for sublicensing may delay or hinder business operations.
- **Market Standard:** Sublicensing is often allowed with notification rather than prior approval to expedite business processes.
- **Negotiability:** High — Lumos should negotiate for notification-based sublicensing rights.
- **Redline:** Modify to "Merck's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, is required for sublicensing, with a provision for notification within 30 days of sublicense agreement execution."
