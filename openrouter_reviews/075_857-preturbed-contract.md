## Executive Summary

This agreement outlines the terms under which the Adviser will provide strategic advice to Gyrodyne, LLC's Board. The Adviser is engaged as an independent contractor with specific confidentiality and indemnification obligations. The agreement includes standard provisions for a Board Adviser role but lacks detailed termination provisions for convenience.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Indemnification** (Section 6)
> "The Company agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Adviser from and against any and all losses..."

- **Issue:** Broad indemnification could expose Adviser to significant liabilities, especially without caps.
- **Risk:** Potential for uncapped financial exposure.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, indemnification is capped.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — advisable to seek a cap on indemnification.
- **Redline:** Add a cap to the indemnification clause.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Termination Provisions** (Section 3)
> "Either party may terminate the Agreement, with or without reason, by written notice."

- **Issue:** Lacks detailed provisions on the consequences of termination for convenience.
- **Market Standard:** Detailed terms including notice period and obligations post-termination.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to request clarity.
- **Redline:** Specify a minimum notice period and duties or obligations upon termination.
