## Executive Summary

This amendment modifies the original employment agreement by reducing the employee's salary by 20% until December 31, 2020, with specific conditions for restoration and penalties for non-acceptance. The amendment also adjusts the definition of "Good Reason" to exclude this salary reduction from triggering a resignation for good reason unless challenged by the employee.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Salary Reduction** (Section 1.4(a))
> "Employee’s salary for the Employment Term ending December 31, 2020, shall be reduced by twenty percent (20%)"

- **Issue:** Significant temporary salary reduction with conditional restoration
- **Risk:** Reduced compensation without clear criteria for restoration
- **Market Standard:** Typically, salary reductions are linked with broader company financial issues and not individual performance
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed
- **Fallback:** Ensure clear criteria for salary restoration are documented

### 🟡 Important

**Amendment to 'Good Reason'** (Section 4.14)
> "Exclusion of the salary reduction from 'Good Reason' unless challenged by the Employee"

- **Issue:** Limits grounds for claiming "Good Reason" for resignation if not challenged
- **Market Standard:** Good Reason typically includes unilateral significant salary reductions
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed
