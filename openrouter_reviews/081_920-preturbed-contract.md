## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Unilateral Amendment Rights** (Section 3(b))
> "The Company may, upon written notice to the Advisor, offset and deduct from any Reimbursement Payment..."

- **Issue:** Allows the Company unilateral rights to amend payment terms under certain conditions without mutual consent.
- **Risk:** Could lead to disputes or unilateral adjustments unfavorable to the Advisor.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, such amendments are mutually agreed upon.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — should seek to modify to require mutual agreement.
- **Redline:** Modify to "Any such amendment shall require mutual written agreement between the Parties."
