## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Limitation on Payments (Section 1.B)**
> "Benefits will be either delivered in full, or to such lesser extent which would result in no portion of such benefits being subject to excise tax..."

- **Issue:** Potential reduction in severance benefits due to tax avoidance provisions.
- **Risk:** Executive might receive reduced benefits to avoid excise taxes.
- **Market Standard:** Full benefits without tax-triggered reductions.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but important for future negotiations.
- **Redline:** N/A (already executed)
- **Fallback:** Seek clarification on scenarios where benefits might be reduced.

---

### 🟡 Important

**COBRA Premium Reimbursement (Section 1.A)**
> "Company will reimburse Executive for the premiums...for a period of six (6) months..."

- **Issue:** Limited duration of COBRA premium support.
- **Market Standard:** 12 months' support is more common in executive agreements.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but consider for future.
- **Redline:** N/A (already executed)
- **Fallback:** In future negotiations, seek extension to 12 months.
