## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 5(d))
> "Purchaser shall pay Seller, as liquidated damages... an amount equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the total purchase price for each such failure, or, at Seller's election, 10,000 USD per day..."

- **Issue:** Extremely punitive liquidated damages clause
- **Risk:** High financial exposure for any breach, regardless of actual damages
- **Market Standard:** More reasonable liquidated damages, typically tied to actual damages
- **Negotiability:** High — should argue for reduction or removal
- **Redline:** Modify to "an amount equal to 10% of the total purchase price or actual damages, whichever is less"
- **Fallback:** Cap at a fixed amount reasonable to potential damages
