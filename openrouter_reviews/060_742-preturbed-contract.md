## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Exhibit A, Paragraph 8)
> "Advisor shall pay the Company as liquidated damages...$50,000 for any breach, plus $5,000 per day..."

- **Issue:** High potential financial liability for breaches
- **Risk:** Significant penalty that could accumulate quickly
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are not this punitive
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but for future, negotiate lower or remove
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements
