## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Lender's Unilateral Disbursement Rights** (Section 3)
> Lender can disburse funds based on its sole discretion if unsatisfactory certificates are provided.

- **Issue:** Broad discretion could lead to disputes or misapplication of funds.
- **Risk:** Guarantor may have limited control over the pledged funds.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, disbursements are more controlled.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — clarification and more specific terms could be negotiated.
- **Redline:** Specify conditions under which Lender can exercise discretion.

### 🟡 Important

**Power of Attorney** (Section 2)
> Full authority granted to Lender to manage and dispose of the account.

- **Issue:** Extensive powers could be misused.
- **Market Standard:** Limited or specific powers of attorney.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to limit scope.
- **Redline:** Restrict powers to actions directly related to loan servicing.
