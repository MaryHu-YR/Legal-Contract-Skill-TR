## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Unilateral Amendment Rights**
> The policy may be amended at any time in the sole discretion of the Board or the Compensation Committee of the Board.

- **Issue:** Allows the company to unilaterally change the policy without director consent.
- **Risk:** Directors have no guarantee that terms won't change unfavorably.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, changes require notice or mutual consent.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — reasonable to request notice or mutual agreement for amendments.
- **Redline:** Suggest adding a clause requiring notice and/or director consent for amendments.
