## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Early Termination Penalties** (Article 8.2)
> "Party B must notify Party A in writing three months in advance, and pay an amount equal to three times the monthly rent as compensation..."

- **Issue:** Harsh penalties for early termination, limiting flexibility.
- **Risk:** High financial cost if needing to exit lease early.
- **Market Standard:** More lenient early termination options.
- **Negotiability:** Low (already executed)
- **Fallback:** N/A

---

### 🟡 Important

**Late Payment Penalties** (Article 5.2)
> "Party B shall pay a late fee of 0.5% of the total rent owed multiplied by number of days late."

- **Issue:** Daily compounding late fee can accumulate quickly.
- **Market Standard:** Fixed late fee or grace period.
- **Negotiability:** Low (already executed)
- **Fallback:** N/A
