## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages / Penalty Provision**
> If the executive breaches the Employment Agreement, they must pay the greater of $661,250, all compensation received, or an amount deemed appropriate by the Company.

- **Issue:** The clause is extremely punitive and lacks standard protections such as notice or opportunity to cure.
- **Risk:** High financial and legal risk if any terms of the agreement are breached, even minimally.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are not this severe and include protections for the employee.
- **Negotiability:** High — this term is unusually harsh and should be moderated.
- **Redline:** Modify to include a cure period and cap the damages to a reasonable amount related to actual loss.
- **Fallback:** If non-negotiable, consider the overall risk vs. reward of accepting the position.
