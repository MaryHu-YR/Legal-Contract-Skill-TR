## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**"As-Is" Condition** (Section 1.2)
> "Premises are leased as-is"

- **Issue:** Tenant accepts the premises in current condition without warranties.
- **Risk:** Potential for unforeseen repair or compliance costs.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, some form of landlord warranty to basic premises standards is provided.
- **Negotiability:** Medium
- **Redline:** Request landlord warranties for compliance with laws and operational condition of critical systems at lease commencement.

### 🟡 Important

**Indemnity** (Section 12.1)
> "Tenant indemnifies landlord..."

- **Issue:** Broad indemnification obligations for the tenant.
- **Market Standard:** Mutual indemnification tailored to fault.
- **Negotiability:** High
- **Redline:** Modify to "Each party indemnifies the other to the extent caused by the indemnifying party's negligence or willful misconduct."
