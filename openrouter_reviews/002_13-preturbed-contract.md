## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Termination Clauses**
> If terminated for cause or voluntary resignation before a monetization event, all incentives are forfeited.

- **Issue:** Harsh forfeiture clauses which could result in loss of all incentives under common termination scenarios.
- **Risk:** High, especially if termination occurs just before a monetization event.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, some prorated incentives might still be payable.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but for future negotiations, seek softer termination penalties.
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements.

### 🟡 Important

**Discretion of the Company**
> The Company may, in its sole discretion, adjust the incentive amount.

- **Issue:** Gives the Company significant control over the final incentive payout.
- **Market Standard:** More transparency and fixed criteria are standard.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — for future agreements, suggest fixed terms based on clear milestones.
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements.
