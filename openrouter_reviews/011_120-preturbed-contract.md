## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Unilateral Amendment Rights** (Contract section)
> "The Company may unilaterally make any amendments it deems necessary or desirable..."

- **Issue:** Allows the Company to change terms without Participant's consent
- **Risk:** Participant has limited control and predictability over the terms of the Award
- **Market Standard:** Typically, amendments require mutual consent
- **Negotiability:** Low — standard in many corporate incentive plans
- **Redline:** Suggest adding a clause requiring Participant's notification and a reasonable objection period

### 🟡 Important

**Termination Provisions** (Termination section)
> "In the event of termination... any RSUs that have not vested will lapse immediately..."

- **Issue:** Harsh for Participant as it allows no grace period or pro-rata vesting
- **Market Standard:** Some pro-rata or grace period vesting upon termination
- **Negotiability:** Medium — depends on the company's policy and negotiation leverage
- **Redline:** Propose a pro-rata vesting schedule or a minimum guaranteed vesting upon termination
