## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Unilateral Amendment Rights** (Amendments Section)
> "The Compensation Committee or the Board of Directors shall review this Policy from time to time..."

- **Issue:** Allows the company to unilaterally adjust compensation without director consent.
- **Risk:** Directors could face reduced compensation retroactively.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, changes require mutual agreement or advance notice.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — reasonable to request advance notice and mutual agreement.
- **Redline:** Specify that changes require "mutual agreement and 60 days' notice."

---

### 🟡 Important

**Make-Whole Payments** (Specific Directors Listed)
> Specific amounts listed for directors as one-time payments.

- **Issue:** Conditional on not being subject to administrative changes.
- **Risk:** Directors might lose entitlements due to unforeseen administrative adjustments.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, such payments are not conditional post factum.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to ask for removal of forfeiture clause.
- **Redline:** Remove "shall forfeit the make-whole payment" and replace with "shall retain regardless of administrative changes unless involved in misconduct."
