## Executive Summary

This agreement outlines the terms under which HAAC Sponsor, LLC will provide office space, utilities, and administrative support to Health Assurance Acquisition Corp. The Company is obligated to pay $10,000 monthly, with additional liquidated damages for non-compliance or unsatisfactory performance.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages**
> "Company shall also pay Sponsor, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an additional amount equal to $25,000 for each such breach or failure..."

- **Issue:** High liquidated damages for unspecified breaches.
- **Risk:** Potential for significant financial liability without clear standards of performance.
- **Market Standard:** More specific performance criteria and reasonable liquidated damages.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — clarification and reduction of damages could be negotiated.
- **Redline:** Specify what constitutes "unsatisfactory performance" and cap the number of times liquidated damages can be applied.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Waiver of Claims Against Trust Account**
> "Sponsor hereby irrevocably waives any and all right, title, interest, causes of action and claims... in or to, and any and all right to seek payment of any amounts due to it out of, the trust account..."

- **Issue:** Broad waiver could limit Sponsor's recourse to essential funds.
- **Market Standard:** Limited waivers specific to certain conditions.
- **Negotiability:** High — ensure waiver is appropriately limited to protect essential assets.
- **Redline:** Narrow the scope of the waiver to claims not involving agreed-upon payments or obligations under this agreement.
