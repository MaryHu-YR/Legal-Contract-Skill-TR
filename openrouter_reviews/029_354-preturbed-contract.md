## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Restricted Stock Grants** (Attachment B)
> "Employee shall forfeit the entire award and shall reimburse Employer, upon demand, for any value, benefit, or economic advantage derived from the grant..."

- **Issue:** Harsh forfeiture and reimbursement conditions if employment terminates before vesting.
- **Risk:** Employee risks losing all granted shares and might have to reimburse the employer if terminated before January 1, 2021.
- **Market Standard:** Forfeiture without reimbursement is standard; reimbursement adds an extra layer of risk.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but for future amendments, seek to remove reimbursement clause.
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements.
