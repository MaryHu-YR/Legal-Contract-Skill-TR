## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 1)
> "Executive shall pay to the Company, as liquidated damages...$250,000 upon the occurrence of such breach, and an additional $1,000 for each day thereafter..."

- **Issue:** High financial risk due to substantial and potentially unlimited liquidated damages.
- **Risk:** Could result in significant financial liability beyond typical employment disputes.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are capped and not structured to accumulate daily.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but future amendments could address.
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements, but consider negotiating caps or limits in future amendments.

### 🟡 Important

**Cumulative Remedies** (Section 2)
> "Any liquidation damages, termination amounts, indemnity payments...shall be cumulative and may be recovered without regard to any cap, offset, mitigation, cure period, or exclusive-remedy provision..."

- **Issue:** Increases financial exposure by allowing cumulative recovery of various penalties.
- **Market Standard:** Remedies typically include caps or are subject to mitigation and cure periods.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but important to address in future negotiations.
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements, but recommend discussing mitigation and cure provisions in future.
