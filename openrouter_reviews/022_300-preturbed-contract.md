## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Forfeiture Clause** (Last paragraph)
> "Vir may immediately require you to forfeit or repay... any benefit, award, or amount Vir deems appropriate..."

- **Issue:** Broad and discretionary power given to Vir to demand forfeiture or repayment of benefits, including equity, without clear standards of unsatisfactory performance.
- **Risk:** High potential for loss of significant financial benefits based on potentially subjective or arbitrary company decisions.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, such clauses are more specific and tied to objective performance metrics or misconduct.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — clarification and limitation of terms could be requested.
- **Redline:** Specify conditions under which forfeiture or repayment can be demanded, ideally linked to measurable performance indicators or serious misconduct.
