## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Vesting and Exercise Conditions**
> Vesting begins on the anniversary of the Grant Date and SARs can only be exercised when vested and if FMV exceeds the Grant Price.

- **Issue:** Conditions for exercise could limit the practical usability of SARs.
- **Risk:** Potential lower benefit if stock price does not exceed the Grant Price significantly.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, SARs are structured to provide meaningful benefits as stock prices increase.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — depends on company policy and specific agreement terms.
- **Redline:** Suggest more favorable exercise conditions or lower Grant Price.

### 🟡 Important

**Termination of Service Provisions**
> SARs can be exercised up to 90 days post-termination unless terminated for Cause.

- **Issue:** Limited window post-termination for exercising SARs.
- **Market Standard:** 90 days is standard, but flexibility varies.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — extension could be requested.
- **Redline:** Extend exercise period post-termination to 180 days for greater flexibility.
