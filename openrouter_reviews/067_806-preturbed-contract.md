## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Forfeiture Clause** (Section 2)
> "All unvested stock will be forfeited if the Director resigns or fails to comply with any provision of this Agreement..."

- **Issue:** Forfeiture for any non-compliance, regardless of materiality or harm caused.
- **Risk:** High risk of losing shares for minor or disputed breaches.
- **Market Standard:** Forfeiture typically applies to material breaches or termination for cause.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — seek to limit to material breaches or provide a cure period.
- **Redline:** Modify to "in the event of a material breach, unremedied for 30 days..."

### 🟡 Important

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 4)
> "Amount equal to the fair market value of all shares..."

- **Issue:** Potentially excessive and automatic penalties for any breach.
- **Market Standard:** Damages usually proportional to the harm caused.
- **Negotiability:** High — such clauses are often negotiable.
- **Redline:** Propose a scaling of damages based on the severity and actual harm of the breach.
