## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Penalty for Non-compliance**
> "The Company shall immediately pay PSC liquidated damages in an amount equal to $25,000 per occurrence, plus an additional $25,000 for each day such failure continues..."

- **Issue:** The penalty clause is unusually punitive and could impose significant financial burdens on the Company for even minor breaches.
- **Risk:** High potential financial exposure without proportionality to the actual harm caused.
- **Market Standard:** More commonly, penalties are scaled or proportional to the breach.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but for future agreements, seek more balanced terms.
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements.

### 🟡 Important

**Waiver of Claims against Trust Account**
> "PSC hereby irrevocably waives any and all right... to seek payment of any amounts due to it out of the trust account..."

- **Issue:** This waiver could limit PSC's recourse to significant assets in case of disputes or defaults.
- **Market Standard:** Waivers are common in trust account structures but should be carefully considered.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but for future agreements, ensure mutual benefit and protection.
- **Redline:** N/A for executed agreements.
