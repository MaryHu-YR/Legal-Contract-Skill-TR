## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Penalties for Reporting Failures** (Section 2)
> "Immediate fee of $250,000 for each failure, plus $25,000 for each Business Day the failure continues."

- **Issue:** High financial risk from strict penalties, regardless of the nature of the reporting failure.
- **Risk:** Significant financial exposure for potentially minor or technical reporting issues.
- **Market Standard:** More lenient penalties or tiered penalties based on the severity of the breach.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — seek to adjust terms to more reasonable penalties or introduce tiered penalties.
- **Redline:** Modify to "Immediate fee of $50,000 for each failure, with an additional $5,000 for each Business Day the failure continues, capped at $100,000 per incident."
