## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Late Charges and Default Rate** (Sections 6 and 7)
- **Issue:** Rates are potentially above market average and could significantly increase financial burden in case of default.
- **Risk:** Excessive penalties could strain financial resources or operational viability.
- **Market Standard:** Late charges typically around 1-2%; default rates usually not exceeding 1% per month.
- **Negotiability:** Low (already executed)
- **Suggested Monitoring:** Ensure timely payments to avoid these charges.

### 🟡 Important

**Automatic Lease Extension** (Section 9(a))
- **Issue:** Lease automatically extends if notice not given 30 days before term ends.
- **Risk:** Unintended extensions could incur unwanted costs.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, extensions require mutual agreement.
- **Negotiability:** Low (already executed)
- **Suggested Action:** Set reminders to provide timely notice.
