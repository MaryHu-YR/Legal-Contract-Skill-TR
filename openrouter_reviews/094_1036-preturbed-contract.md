## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Unilateral Changes and Forfeiture** (Sections 6.1, 6.2, 7.0)
> "The Compensation Committee reserves the right to change, suspend or eliminate this Plan, in whole or in part, at any time..."

- **Issue:** The company retains the right to unilaterally modify or terminate the incentive plan without notice.
- **Risk:** Potential loss of expected bonuses and lack of recourse for the employee.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, such plans are subject to mutual agreement and notice.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — clarification and limitation of these rights can be requested.
- **Redline:** Modify to include a notice period and mutual agreement for any changes to the plan.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Bonus Eligibility and Payment Terms** (Sections 4.1, 4.4)
> "Incentive awards are subject to approval... and may be withheld, reduced or clawed back..."

- **Issue:** Conditional bonus payments based on company's discretion post-performance period.
- **Market Standard:** Conditional bonuses are standard but typically with predefined criteria.
- **Negotiability:** High — seek to define clearer performance metrics and payout conditions.
- **Redline:** Specify exact conditions under which bonuses can be withheld or reduced.
