## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 1A)
> "Liquidated damages in an amount equal to $5,000,000 per day..."

- **Issue:** Extremely high liquidated damages, irrespective of actual harm or materiality of breach
- **Risk:** Could impose severe financial penalties on Tenants for minor breaches
- **Market Standard:** More reasonable, proportional liquidated damages
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but would typically be high if pre-execution
- **Redline:** Suggest more reasonable, harm-proportional damages in future amendments

---

### 🟡 Important

**Distribution Frequency Change** (Section 1)
> "Interim distributions...shall be made twice per Portfolio Accounting Period..."

- **Issue:** Increases administrative burden and complexity
- **Market Standard:** Typically once per accounting period
- **Negotiability:** Medium — depends on operational capacity
- **Redline:** Consider reverting to standard practice if operational issues arise
