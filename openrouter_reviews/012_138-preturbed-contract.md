## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Severance Compensation**
> Enhanced severance benefits including base salary continuation and health benefits under certain termination conditions.

- **Issue:** Conditional on signing a release of claims, which might limit legal recourse.
- **Risk:** Could waive potential claims against the company.
- **Market Standard:** Common in executive agreements but requires careful consideration.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — Clarification and potential adjustment of release terms.
- **Redline:** Specify or limit the scope of claims being released.

### 🟡 Important

**Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation**
> These agreements remain in effect and unaltered.

- **Issue:** Restrictions might be overly broad or long-term.
- **Market Standard:** Typically 1-2 years post-employment.
- **Negotiability:** High — if terms are excessively restrictive.
- **Redline:** Review and potentially renegotiate the scope or duration of restrictions.
