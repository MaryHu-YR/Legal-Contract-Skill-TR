## Executive Summary

This agreement outlines the provision of office space, utilities, and administrative support by Constellation Alpha Holdings LLC to Alpha Healthcare Acquisition Corp. The agreement is effective from the listing date of the company's securities until the termination of the agreement, either through a business combination or company liquidation. The terms include a fixed monthly payment and penalties for late payments, with specific waivers regarding claims against the company's trust account.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Late Payment Penalties** (Section (i))
> "If the Company fails to make any such payment when due for any reason, the Company shall immediately owe Constellation an additional amount equal to $15,000 for each month or partial month during which such failure continues."

- **Issue:** High penalty for late payments which could accumulate significantly.
- **Risk:** Financial strain in case of delayed payments.
- **Market Standard:** Late fees exist, but $15,000 per month is steep.
- **Negotiability:** Low (already executed)
- **Redline:** N/A (already executed)

### 🟡 Important

**Waiver of Claims Against Trust Account** (Section (ii))
