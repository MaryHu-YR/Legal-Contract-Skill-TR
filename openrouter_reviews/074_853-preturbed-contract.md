## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 7)
> "If any party fails to comply...shall pay the other parties, as liquidated damages...$5,000,000 for each such failure or breach, plus $100,000 for each day the failure or breach continues..."

- **Issue:** Extremely high liquidated damages could impose significant financial burden even for minor breaches.
- **Risk:** Disproportionate to potential harm, potentially unenforceable as a penalty.
- **Market Standard:** More reasonable and proportionate damages.
- **Negotiability:** Low (already executed)
- **Redline:** N/A (for future agreements, suggest recalibrating to actual damages or a more reasonable estimate)
