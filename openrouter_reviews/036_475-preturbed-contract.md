## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages Clause**
> "Company shall immediately pay Provider the full monthly amount as liquidated damages for such failure, regardless of whether Provider actually incurs any loss"

- **Issue:** Clause imposes strict liquidated damages for non-payment, potentially harsh given the fixed nature of the fee.
- **Risk:** Could lead to significant financial liability without actual loss on the Provider's part.
- **Market Standard:** More commonly, damages are tied to actual loss.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could argue for damages based on actual loss.
- **Redline:** Modify to reflect damages based on actual loss incurred by Provider.

### 🟡 Important

**Waiver of Claims Against Trust Account**
> "Provider irrevocably waives any Claim it may have in the future as a result of, or arising out of, any negotiations, contracts or agreements with the Company"

- **Issue:** Broad waiver could limit Provider's recourse in unforeseen circumstances.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, waivers are more specific and limited.
- **Negotiability:** High — specificity can be increased to protect Provider's interests.
- **Redline:** Limit waiver to claims directly related to the use of funds in the Trust Account.
