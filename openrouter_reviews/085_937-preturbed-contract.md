## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Interest Rate Fluctuations** (Sections 2.7, 2.8)
> "Interest rates are variable based on certain benchmarks."

- **Issue:** Exposure to rate increases if benchmarks rise
- **Risk:** Increased financial burden during rate hikes
- **Market Standard:** Typically includes caps or swaps to manage risk
- **Negotiability:** Medium — hedging options might be negotiable
- **Redline:** Include interest rate cap or hedge provisions

---

### 🟡 Important

**Commitment Fees and Unused Lines** (Section 2.12, 2.13)
> "Fees associated with the commitment and unused lines of credit."

- **Issue:** Costs incurred even when the credit line is not fully utilized
- **Market Standard:** Common in credit agreements but rates vary
- **Negotiability:** Medium — possible to negotiate lower fees or thresholds
- **Redline:** Reduce or tier the unused commitment fees based on usage
