### 🔴 Critical

**Indemnification of Tenant** (Section G)
> "Landlord shall defend, indemnify and hold Tenant harmless from and against all liabilities..."

- **Issue:** Broad indemnification could impose significant financial burden on Landlord.
- **Risk:** High potential costs from legal actions or lease violations by Landlord.
- **Market Standard:** Typically more limited scope.
