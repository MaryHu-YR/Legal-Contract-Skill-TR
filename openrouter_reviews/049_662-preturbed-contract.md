## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Paragraph 3)
> "The Shareholder shall pay to the Company and the Holder, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to the greater of (i) $250,000 for each such breach or violation, whether material or immaterial, and (ii) $25,000 per day for so long as any such breach, failure to comply, or violation continues or is discovered..."

- **Issue:** High penalties which could accumulate significantly, imposing substantial financial burden without the need to prove actual damages.
- **Risk:** Severe financial exposure for the shareholder.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are not this punitive and are proportional to the actual harm caused.
- **Negotiability:** Low — Given the executed status, renegotiation might not be possible.
- **Redline:** N/A due to executed status.
- **Fallback:** Seek legal advice to explore any potential for relief under applicable law if terms are deemed unconscionable.
