## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Unused Revolving Credit Commitment Fee** (Section 3(b))
> "The Borrower shall pay to the Lender a commitment fee at the rate per annum equal to 0.075% of the daily amount by which the face amount of the Revolving Note exceeds the principal amount of Revolving Loans then outstanding."

- **Issue:** Fee imposed for unutilized credit could accumulate significantly.
- **Risk:** Financial burden without direct benefit from the funds.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, non-usage fees are lower or negotiable.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — possible to negotiate a lower rate or waiver.
- **Redline:** Propose a reduction in the fee or a cap on the total non-usage fees payable.

### 🟡 Important

**Renewal Fee** (Section 4)
> "Payment by CAS to Lender of a renewal fee in the amount of $25,000.00."

- **Issue:** High one-time fee for the renewal of terms.
- **Market Standard:** Renewal fees are common but usually lower.
- **Negotiability:** High — should negotiate a reduction or justify the fee with additional benefits.
- **Redline:** Reduce the fee to $10,000 or negotiate additional credit terms in exchange for the fee.
