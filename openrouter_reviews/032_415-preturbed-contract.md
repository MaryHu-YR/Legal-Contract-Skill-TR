## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Pro Rata Bonus Calculation** (Section 7.1)
> Adjustments to the bonus calculation allow the Company discretion to reduce the bonus for any perceived breaches or unsatisfactory performance without a requirement to demonstrate actual loss.

- **Issue:** Broad discretion could lead to subjective reductions in bonus payments.
- **Risk:** Potential for reduced compensation without clear, objective standards.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, bonus reductions are tied to specific, demonstrable metrics or losses.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — Clarification on standards for reductions could be requested.
- **Redline:** Specify examples of breaches or performance issues that might lead to reductions.

### 🟡 Important

**Voluntary Bonus Reduction** (Final Paragraph)
> Executive's voluntary reduction of bonus does not constitute a "Good Reason" for severance claims.

- **Issue:** Limits Executive's ability to claim severance under reduced bonus scenarios.
- **Market Standard:** Good Reason typically includes unilateral reductions in compensation.
- **Negotiability:** High — Reasonable to request removal or clarification of this clause.
- **Redline:** Remove or clarify that only reductions beyond the Executive's control qualify as Good Reason.
