## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages Clause**
> "If the Company fails to timely pay any amount due under this Agreement or otherwise fails to comply with any provision of this Agreement, the Sponsor may require the Company to pay liquidated damages in an amount equal to $50,000 for each such failure."

- **Issue:** High liquidated damages may be disproportionate to actual harm.
- **Risk:** Could impose significant financial burden for minor breaches.
- **Market Standard:** More typical liquidated damages are calculated based on actual losses.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but worth noting for future agreements.
- **Redline:** Suggest recalculating damages based on actual harm in future agreements.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Waiver of Claims Against Trust Account**
> "The Sponsor hereby irrevocably waives any and all right, title, interest, causes of action and claims of any kind as a result of, or arising out of, this Agreement in or to the trust account..."

- **Issue:** Waiver could limit Sponsor's recourse in case of disputes.
- **Market Standard:** Uncommon to waive such broad rights unconditionally.
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but a potential concern.
- **Redline:** Future agreements should consider more balanced provisions regarding claim waivers.
