## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**General Release and Covenant Not to Sue** (Section 5)
> Employee releases all claims against the company and agrees not to sue.

- **Issue:** Broad waiver of rights, including discrimination and employment claims.
- **Risk:** Limits legal recourse for past grievances.
- **Market Standard:** Common in separation agreements but requires careful consideration.
- **Negotiability:** Low — standard but ensure understanding and fairness.
- **Redline:** N/A due to execution status.

### 🟡 Important

**Confidentiality** (Section 4)
> Employee must keep all company information confidential indefinitely.

- **Issue:** Indefinite duration for confidentiality.
- **Market Standard:** Typically limited to a reasonable period post-employment unless involving trade secrets.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could request time-bound except for trade secrets.
- **Redline:** Suggest limiting to 3-5 years post-termination for non-trade secret information.
