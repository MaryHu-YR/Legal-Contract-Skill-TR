## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Financial Commitments** (Section 1)
> "A transaction fee of $15 million, payable at or promptly following the closing of a Transaction."

- **Issue:** High financial commitment which could impact cash flow.
- **Risk:** Large outlay of funds upon transaction closing.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, advisory fees are substantial but aligned with industry norms.
- **Negotiability:** Low — Standard in financial advisory agreements.
- **Redline:** N/A — Standard provision.

### 🟡 Important

**Purchase Commitment** (Section 1)
> "Advisor shall purchase shares up to $15 million if not purchased from third parties during the Purchasing Period."

- **Issue:** Obligates Advisor to make substantial purchases, potentially at unfavorable market conditions.
- **Market Standard:** Uncommon to have such specific stock purchase commitments tied to advisory services.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — Could potentially adjust terms or conditions.
- **Redline:** Modify to provide more flexibility in terms of market conditions or timing.
