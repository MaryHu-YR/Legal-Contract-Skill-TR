## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Minimum Liquidity Requirements** (Clause 19.2(b)(i))
> "Minimum Liquidity: Notwithstanding the provisions in Clause 25.1(d)(ii) hereof, at all times it maintains Consolidated Liquidity at least equal to the higher of (A) $27,500,000 and (B) 5% of consolidated interest bearing debt outstanding of the Facility Guarantor and its Subsidiaries..."

- **Issue:** High liquidity requirements could strain financial resources.
- **Risk:** Non-compliance results in severe penalties.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, less stringent conditions.
- **Negotiability:** Low — given the number of involved parties and the nature of the agreement.
- **Redline:** Seek to lower the liquidity threshold or extend compliance timelines.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Deletion of Clause 28.19 (Arrest of Ship)**
> Replaced with "28.19. Intentionally Omitted"

- **Issue:** Removal of protections related to ship arrest.
- **Risk:** Increased vulnerability in maritime operations.
- **Market Standard:** Specific protections typically included.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — possible to argue for reinstatement based on industry standards.
- **Redline:** Propose a revised clause that offers some level of protection against ship arrest.
