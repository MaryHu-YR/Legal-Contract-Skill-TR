## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Put Option** (Section 2)
> Seller is granted a put option to sell up to $2,000,000 of shares to the Company.

- **Issue:** Put option introduces potential financial risk depending on market conditions.
- **Risk:** Market price fluctuations could affect the desirability and financial impact of exercising the put option.
- **Market Standard:** Conditional put options are common in such agreements but require careful structuring.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — terms might be adjusted based on market forecasts and financial projections.
- **Redline:** Consider specifying a minimum threshold for the market price to safeguard against unfavorable market conditions.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Confidentiality** (Section 6)
> Seller must not disclose the existence or contents of this Agreement without prior consent.

- **Issue:** Restrictive confidentiality clause without standard exceptions (e.g., for legal advice).
- **Market Standard:** Include exceptions for disclosures required by law, regulatory requirements, or for obtaining professional advice.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to request standard exceptions.
- **Redline:** Modify to allow disclosures as required by law or for obtaining professional advice without prior consent.
