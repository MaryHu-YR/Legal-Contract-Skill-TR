## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Default Penalties** (Section 12)
> "Borrower shall immediately pay Lender, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to the greater of (i) five percent (5%) of the entire outstanding principal balance or (ii) $2,500 per occurrence..."

- **Issue:** High default penalties which could significantly increase financial burden.
- **Risk:** Excessive costs in case of minor administrative lapses.
- **Market Standard:** More lenient penalties or opportunities to cure without severe penalties.
- **Negotiability:** Low — lender-driven, standard for SBA loans.
- **Redline:** Seek to reduce penalty or introduce a cure period before penalties apply.

### 🟡 Important

**Lender's General Powers** (Section 6)
> "Lender may incur expenses to collect amounts due under this Note, enforce the terms of this Note or any other Loan Document, and preserve or dispose of the Collateral."

- **Issue:** Broad powers granted to Lender without Borrower's consent.
- **Market Standard:** Typically more balanced with notice or mutual agreement.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — possible to negotiate notifications or limitations.
- **Redline:** Propose amendments to require lender to notify borrower before taking significant actions.
