## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Financial Obligations and Penalties** (Section 3)
> Various uncapped financial obligations related to production delays and minimum order requirements.

- **Issue:** Potential high financial exposure due to penalties for delays and idle fees.
- **Risk:** Significant costs if OPN fails to meet order requirements or delays occur.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, such penalties are capped.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — should negotiate caps on penalties and fees.
- **Redline:** Propose caps on delay penalties and idle fees.

### 🟡 Important

**Covid Claim Resolution** (Section 2)
> "VTM agrees that no amount shall be due in connection with the Covid Claim..."

- **Issue:** Resolution may not cover future claims or similar unforeseen events.
- **Market Standard:** Clarity on handling of similar future claims.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to clarify handling of future unforeseen costs.
- **Redline:** Add provisions for handling similar claims in the future.
