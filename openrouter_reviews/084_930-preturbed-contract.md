## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Severance Benefits** (Section 2)
> Various severance benefits based on the type of termination.

- **Issue:** Complex and varied severance terms could lead to misunderstandings or disputes.
- **Risk:** Potential for disputes over severance calculations and eligibility.
- **Market Standard:** Clear and consistent severance terms.
- **Negotiability:** Medium
- **Redline:** Simplify and clarify severance terms to ensure consistency and understanding.

### 🟡 Important

**Change in Control Provisions** (Sections 1, 2(c))
> Adjustments to bonuses and severance payments upon a change in control.

- **Issue:** Provisions are complex and could lead to disputes regarding the exact entitlements.
- **Market Standard:** Clear and straightforward change in control provisions.
- **Negotiability:** High
- **Redline:** Clarify the provisions to ensure they are easily understandable and implementable.
