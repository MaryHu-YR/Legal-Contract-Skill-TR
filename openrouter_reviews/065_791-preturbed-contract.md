## Executive Summary

This amendment allows the Executive to defer up to 50% of his base salary from May to December 2020, with the option to request payment at any time, ensuring all deferred salary is paid by March 15, 2021. It includes a penalty for late payment by the Company, equal to 100% of the affected deferred salary.
