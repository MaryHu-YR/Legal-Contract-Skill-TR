## Executive Summary

This agreement outlines the provision of office space and administrative services by the Sponsor to the Company, with specific financial obligations and penalties for non-compliance. The Sponsor waives any claims against the Company's trust account. The terms are somewhat one-sided favoring the Sponsor, particularly concerning liquidated damages and unilateral modifications.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 1)
> "The Company shall pay the Sponsor, as liquidated damages...an additional amount of $10,000 for each such failure..."

- **Issue:** High penalty regardless of the nature or seriousness of the breach.
- **Risk:** Could lead to significant financial liability for minor breaches.
- **Market Standard:** More tailored liquidated damages clauses.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — should seek to limit or specify conditions.
- **Redline:** Specify conditions under which liquidated damages apply and cap the total amount.

### 🟡 Important

**Unilateral Amendment Rights** (Section 1)
> "The payment obligation...may be waived or modified by the Company at any time without notice to the Sponsor..."

- **Issue:** Allows the Company to unilaterally modify payment terms.
- **Market Standard:** Mutual agreement required for amendments.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to require mutual consent.
- **Redline:** Require written consent from both parties for any amendments.
