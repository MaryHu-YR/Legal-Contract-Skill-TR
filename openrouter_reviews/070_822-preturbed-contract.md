## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Non-Solicitation Clause** (Section 2)
> "The Executive agrees, during the period in which she is employed by the Company and for one (1) year thereafter, not to solicit any person who is an employee of, or a consultant or independent contractor to, the Company."

- **Issue:** Restricts post-employment activities, potentially limiting future employment opportunities.
- **Market Standard:** Typically 1-2 years, but enforceability varies by jurisdiction.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could request a narrower scope or shorter duration.
- **Redline:** Reduce the duration to six (6) months or limit the scope to employees directly managed by the Executive.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Incentive Compensation Conditions** (Section 1(a))
> "Payments are contingent upon being employed by the Company at the time of payment."

- **Issue:** Payment is forfeited if not employed at the time of milestones, regardless of contribution to achievements.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, pro-rated payments or allowances for termination without cause.
- **Negotiability:** High — reasonable to request pro-rated compensation if termination is without cause.
- **Redline:** Modify to allow pro-rated payment if terminated without cause before the payment date.
