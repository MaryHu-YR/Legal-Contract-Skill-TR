## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Utilization Fee** (New Exhibit M)
> "Utilization fee of $250,000 for any failure to comply with this Agreement..."

- **Issue:** Fee accrues daily for each noncompliance event, potentially unlimited
- **Risk:** Severe financial exposure without regard to actual damages
- **Market Standard:** Typically, penalties are capped and tied to actual damages
- **Negotiability:** Low — already executed, but discuss for future amendments
- **Redline:** N/A (already executed)
- **Fallback:** In future negotiations, propose caps and ties to actual damages
