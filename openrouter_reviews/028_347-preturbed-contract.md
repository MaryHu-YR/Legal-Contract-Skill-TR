## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Lockup Period** (Section 1)
> "Stockholder shall not sell, offer to sell, hypothecate, pledge, or dispose of the Shares..."

- **Issue:** Restricts liquidity of Stockholder's shares for 180 days, potentially longer if extended by Peck.
- **Risk:** Limits financial flexibility and market responsiveness of Stockholder.
- **Market Standard:** 180 days is standard, but unilateral extension by Peck is aggressive.
- **Negotiability:** Low — typical in merger scenarios but extension terms could be negotiated.
- **Redline:** Propose fixed end date for Lockup Period without extension rights.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Transfer Restrictions** (Section 2)
> "Stockholder may transfer shares within immediate family or to trusts for estate planning..."

- **Issue:** Transfers are tightly controlled, even within family or for estate planning.
- **Market Standard:** Common in lockup agreements but often more flexible.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — seek to ease restrictions for personal planning needs.
- **Redline:** Broaden permissible transfer definitions or reduce procedural burdens.
