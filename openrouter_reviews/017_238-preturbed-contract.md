## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Unilateral Amendment Rights**
> The General Partner has the authority to unilaterally amend the Partnership Agreement to reflect changes in ownership structure.

- **Issue:** Grants significant power to the General Partner, potentially at the expense of Limited Partners
- **Risk:** Limited Partners may have reduced influence over material changes
- **Market Standard:** Typically, such amendments require broader partner approval
- **Negotiability:** Medium — could push for a requirement of limited partner consent for future amendments
- **Redline:** Propose adding a clause requiring limited partner consent for amendments affecting partner rights
