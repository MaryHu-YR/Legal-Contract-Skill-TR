## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 14)
> "In the event of any breach...you shall pay the Company...an amount equal to twelve (12) months of your then-current base salary..."

- **Issue:** Extremely punitive and potentially unenforceable as it does not allow for a cure period and is enforceable regardless of actual harm.
- **Risk:** High financial risk if employment is terminated under contentious circumstances.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, liquidated damages are not this severe in employment contracts.
- **Negotiability:** High — should argue for removal or significant reduction.
- **Redline:** Remove or modify to allow for cure periods and tie to actual damages.

### 🟡 Important

**Unilateral Amendment Rights** (Section 1)
> "The Company reserves the right to change your title and responsibilities at any time, with or without notice."

- **Issue:** Allows the Company to alter fundamental aspects of employment unilaterally.
- **Market Standard:** Changes usually discussed and agreed upon, not unilaterally imposed.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — reasonable to request mutual agreement on changes.
- **Redline:** Modify to "changes subject to mutual agreement."
