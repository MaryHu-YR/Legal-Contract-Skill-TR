## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Offshore Jurisdiction** (Preamble)
> "Antara Capital Master Fund LP, a Cayman Islands exempted limited partnership"

- **Issue:** Jurisdiction may complicate enforcement and increase litigation costs
- **Risk:** Potential difficulties in legal recourse
- **Market Standard:** Prefer jurisdictions with more predictable legal environments
- **Negotiability:** Low — established in initial agreement
- **Redline:** Consider future agreements with entities in more favorable jurisdictions

---

### 🟡 Important

**Liquidated Damages** (Section 2)
> "The Payment represents the agreed upon amount of liquidated damages due or to become due to the Investor by the Company under Section 2.02 of the RRA..."

- **Issue:** High amount could indicate severe penalties for non-compliance
- **Market Standard:** Liquidated damages are typical but should be reasonable and not punitive
- **Negotiability:** Medium — review reasonableness and potential for reduction in future amendments
- **Redline:** Ensure clarity on calculation and conditions for liquidated damages in future agreements
