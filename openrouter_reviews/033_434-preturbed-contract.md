## Executive Summary

This Leak-Out Agreement is designed to control the sale of shares acquired under an Exchange Agreement, imposing restrictions on the volume of shares that can be sold within a specified period. The agreement includes provisions for automatic adjustments in favor of the Holder if more favorable terms are offered to other holders.

## Risk Analysis

### 🔴 Critical

**Sale Restrictions**
> "Neither the Holder nor any of its Affiliates shall sell, on any trading day, more than 10% of the Common Stock sold on such trading day."

- **Issue:** Restricts liquidity of the investment by limiting daily sales volume.
- **Risk:** Could impact the Holder's ability to exit the investment efficiently.
- **Market Standard:** Typically, such restrictions are not as stringent.
- **Negotiability:** Medium — possible to negotiate higher daily limits or shorter restricted period.
- **Redline:** Propose increasing daily sale limit to 20% or reducing the restricted period to 90 days.

---

### 🟡 Important

**Favorable Terms Adjustment Clause**
> "Automatically amended and modified in an economically and legally equivalent manner such that the Holder shall receive the benefit of the more favorable terms."

- **Issue:** While protective, depends on the Company's transparency and compliance.
- **Market Standard:** Not common; typically, each agreement stands on its own.
- **Negotiability:** Low — beneficial but complex to enforce.
- **Redline:** Clarify the mechanisms for enforcement and notification.
